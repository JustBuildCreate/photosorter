PhotoSorter

Personal Windows app for a curated photo screensaver album

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

PhotoSorter runs on your PC. It scores and organizes photos from local libraries (for example iCloud and disk folders), builds a curated screensaver pool, and can sync that pool to a Google Photos album that you control.

It is intended for personal / household use by the developer and people they know.

Google Photos access

  • Sign-in uses Google OAuth on this machine (desktop app flow).
  • PhotoSorter only uses Photos Library scopes needed to manage an app-created album and related cleanup album.
  • Your photo library is not sent to third-party vision/cloud AI APIs for scoring; local models run on the PC.
  • The OAuth token is stored locally next to the app data folder.
