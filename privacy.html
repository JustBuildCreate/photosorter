Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

PhotoSorter (“the app”) is a personal desktop application. This policy describes how it handles information when you use Google sign-in and Google Photos sync.

Who the app is for

The app is operated for personal / household use by the developer (GitHub: JustBuildCreate) and a small number of people known to them. It is not offered as a public multi-tenant service.

Data the app accesses

  • Local photos: files you configure as sources on your computer (read to score/copy; originals are not deleted by the app).
  • Google Account (OAuth): when you choose Sign in, Google provides tokens so the app can call the Photos Library API with the scopes you approve.
  • Google Photos (app-created data): the app can create and update albums and media items that this app created (screensaver album and related cleanup album). It does not request full library browse of all your Photos.

How Google user data is used

Google Photos data is used only to:

  • Upload curated screensaver images into an app-created album
  • Add/remove items in that album to match the local pool
  • Maintain a separate app-created “trash me” cleanup album for items leaving the pool

The app does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for unrelated purposes.

Storage

  • OAuth client config and refresh/access tokens are stored in the app’s local data folder on your PC (for example PhotoSorterData).
  • Local SQLite state tracks which photos are in the pool and which Google media IDs the app created.
  • Scoring uses local AI models on your machine; your photo library is not uploaded to third-party cloud vision APIs.

Sharing

Aside from the Google APIs you authorize, the app does not transmit your Google Photos content to other online services. Network use for Google sync goes to Google’s Photos Library API endpoints.

Retention and deletion

  • You can Sign out in the app to delete the local Google token file.
  • You can revoke access at Google Account permissions.
  • App-created albums/items may remain in your Google Photos until you delete them in the Photos UI (or via the app’s normal sync/cleanup flow).

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the public site repo github.com/JustBuildCreate/photosorter or contact the developer via the email configured on the Google Cloud OAuth consent screen.
